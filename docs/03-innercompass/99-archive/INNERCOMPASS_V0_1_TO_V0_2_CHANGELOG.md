# InnerCompass — Question Draft Changes V0.1.0 → V0.2.0

The previous V0.1.0 question bank is retained unchanged. V0.2.0 is a separate revision with new version-specific IDs (`icv02-01` to `icv02-30`).

| Area | Main change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 | Renamed to “ယုံကြည်စိတ်ချရမှုနှင့် တာဝန်ခံမှု”; softened overtly moral language. | Reduce the impression that the score rates someone's moral worth. |
| IC2 | Reworded emotional awareness and support-seeking prompts. | Separate noticing feelings from describing them; avoid assuming a trusted person is always available. |
| IC3 | Renamed to “လူမှုဆက်ဆံရေးတွင် ကိုယ့်ကိုယ်ကို ဖော်ပြပုံ”. | Avoid framing quietness or introversion as a deficit. |
| IC4 | Reworded disagreement and compromise prompts. | Avoid implying that a person must agree or compromise in every situation. |
| IC5 | Reworded task continuation and organisation prompts. | Use more observable everyday actions. |
| IC6 | Clarified curiosity, aesthetic noticing, and small experimentation. | Avoid equating aesthetic appreciation with artistic skill or novelty with automatic acceptance. |

## Reverse-keyed items

V0.1.0 IDs: `ic04`, `ic10`, `ic14`, `ic19`, `ic25`, `ic29`.

V0.2.0 IDs: `icv02-04`, `icv02-10`, `icv02-14`, `icv02-19`, `icv02-25`, `icv02-29`.

These items remain provisional. Reverse wording can reduce simple agreement patterns but can also increase confusion. Keep them only if cognitive interviews show that they are understood as intended.

## Compatibility

V0.2.0 is not activated in the application and does not replace V0.1.0. When an approved future version is activated, saved attempts must record the exact question-bank version so text is never silently changed mid-assessment.
