# InnerCompass — Editorial Review V0.2.0

## Review status

This is an internal editorial review of the revised draft. It is **not** a psychologist's review, expert validation, cognitive-interview result, pilot report, or psychometric validation.

## What changed from V0.1.0

- Reduced moralising language in the first area and renamed it to “ယုံကြည်စိတ်ချရမှုနှင့် တာဝန်ခံမှု”.
- Changed the social area label so quietness is not implicitly treated as a deficit.
- Clarified items that were vague, awkward, or overly broad.
- Made the disagreement area less likely to imply that compromise is always required.
- Added context-sensitive wording where access to support or opportunities may affect responses.
- Kept a separate archived V0.1.0 bank; V0.2.0 uses version-specific question IDs.

## Reverse-keying review

Current reverse-keyed IDs:

- `icv02-04`
- `icv02-10`
- `icv02-14`
- `icv02-19`
- `icv02-25`
- `icv02-29`

Reverse wording can reduce simple agreement patterns but may also create confusion, especially when a statement is long or negated. Cognitive interviews must confirm comprehension before retaining these items. Do not assume that six reverse items guarantee response-quality control.

## Structure and duplication checks

- Six original reflection areas.
- Five items per area.
- Thirty unique IDs and thirty unique question strings.
- One item for each of five themes in each area.
- Six reverse-keyed items, one in each area.
- No question repeats the exact same wording.

These checks confirm structure only. They do not demonstrate that the six areas are empirically distinct, internally consistent, or valid.
