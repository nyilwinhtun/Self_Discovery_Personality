# InnerCompass — Initial Burmese Item Review V0

## Status

This is an editorial review of a draft bank, not an expert validation or psychometric report. The 30 prompts are not active in the public application.

## Balance check

- Six original reflection areas.
- Five questions per area.
- Thirty unique IDs and thirty unique text strings.
- One reverse-keyed item in each area, for an initial total of six reverse-keyed items.
- Five-point agreement response scale.

## Items for careful cognitive review

| Item | Review concern | What to check with Burmese readers |
|---|---|---|
| ic02 | “မမျှတတဲ့ ဆုံးဖြတ်ချက်” may be interpreted differently by family, workplace, or community context. | Ask what kind of decision the reader imagines; ensure it does not sound like a legal or moral test. |
| ic04 | Reverse-keyed item about leaving out information may invite socially desirable responding. | Check whether readers understand the everyday situation without feeling accused. |
| ic08 | Help-seeking can depend on whether a safe, trusted person is available. | Check that the item is interpreted as a personal response tendency, not as a judgement about people who lack support. |
| ic10 | Recovery after an upsetting event can depend on event severity and context. | Ensure it does not imply that a person should recover quickly or suppress feelings. |
| ic14 | Public speaking comfort can be influenced by language, status, disability, or context. | Ensure a lower response is not described as a defect or poor social ability. |
| ic19 | Reconsidering one's view is not always appropriate when the other person's claim is unsupported or unsafe. | Check that the item measures openness to reconsidering, not pressure to agree. |
| ic25 | The draft wording is a reverse-keyed everyday organisation item. | Ask whether “ပစ္စည်းတွေ ဘယ်နေရာမှာထားခဲ့သလဲ မကြာခဏ မေ့တတ်တယ်” is clearer and less double-negative. |
| ic28 | Enjoying art or nature is not the same as creative ability. | Keep the interpretation limited to noticing and enjoying aesthetic experiences. |
| ic29 | Rejecting unfamiliar ideas may be a result of context or prior evidence. | Ensure the item is not interpreted as requiring acceptance of every new idea. |
| ic30 | Trying an idea in practice can depend on time, money, and opportunity. | Ensure answers are interpreted as general preference, not access to resources. |

## Recommended wording check for ic25

Current initial draft wording (updated before first activation):

> ကိုယ်အသုံးပြုတဲ့ ပစ္စည်းတွေကို ဘယ်နေရာမှာ ထားခဲ့သလဲဆိုတာ မကြာခဏ မေ့တတ်တယ်။

This wording is clearer for an everyday example, but it still needs cognitive review. Any later change after users have active drafts must increment the question-bank version so existing drafts retain their original text.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Area 1 must not become a moral-worth score.
- Area 2 must not become a mental-health or trauma screen.
- Area 3 must not treat quietness or introversion as a weakness.
- Area 4 must not recommend tolerating harm or abandoning reasonable boundaries.
- Area 5 must not equate organisation with personal worth.
- Area 6 must not assume everyone has equal access to art, travel, education, or experimentation.
